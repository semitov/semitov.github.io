# RISC-V Summit Europe 2026

In June 2026 SemiTO-V will present its latest project developments at RISC-V Summit Europe 2026 in Bologna, Italy. We'll await you at our booth in the developers demo area to show you our latest our very own electronic badge, featuring the team's very own RISC-V processor based on the RV32IM instruction set, along with a RP2350, a screen and buttons to interact with it. It will a fun experience to write your assembly program and watch it execute on the core, realised inside of an FPGA.
