# World Days RISC-V 2025

In February 2025, our team organized hosted the World Days RISC-V in Politecnico di Torino. It was a great opportunity to showcase our work and meet other RISC-V enthusiasts. As well as a chance to learn from our esteemed guests and collegues working on RISC-V.
