# World RISC-V Days Turin 2026

On February 26, 2026, our team organized hosted Turin edition of World RISC-V Days in Politecnico di Torino. It was a great opportunity to showcase our work and meet other RISC-V enthusiasts, as well as a chance to learn from our esteemed guests and collegues working on RISC-V. 

We recorded the talks and shared them on our YouTube channel. [Watch here](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLd1g_v8LLtSxhPXkLj_FIH_Y481nRpMWd)!
